I am a Computer Science PhD student at North Carolina State University, advised by Professor Ranga Raju Vatsavai. My research focuses on geospatial machine learning, remote sensing, semantic segmentation, and computer vision. I expect to complete my PhD in December 2027 and previously earned my MCS and BS in Computer Science from NC State.

My work connects reproducible machine-learning systems with practical environmental applications. From August 2023 through July 2026, I worked as a Data Scientist in Geospatial ML and Remote Sensing at Natrx, developing coastal-analysis workflows from satellite imagery, LiDAR, and other spatial datasets.
